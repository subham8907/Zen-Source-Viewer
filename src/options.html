Sites this add-on may contact Every site is off until you turn it on. Zen Source Viewer only connects to a site that is turned on, and only when you open an add-on from it. A site that is off is never contacted: its pages are not recognized and requests to it are blocked. Other addresses you paste yourself are not affected.
